Overlay layouts

Live previews using whatever is playing right now. Pick one, copy its URL, and paste it into an OBS Browser Source with the listed size. The checkered background is only here to judge legibility — in OBS the overlay is transparent.

Mix in any of these: ?accent=ff0055 · theme=solid · align=right · valign=top · scale=1.25 · showAlbum=1 · showArt=0 · hideWhenPaused=1 · speed=90 (ticker). Full list is in the README.
