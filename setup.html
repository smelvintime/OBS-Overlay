Set up Twitch

Follower and subscriber alerts, live goal boxes and the chat bot all need permission to read your channel. This page gets that permission once — after it, everything renews itself automatically. Nothing here ever leaves this computer.

1Your channel and your Twitch app

The overlay talks to Twitch as an "application" that you own. Creating one is free, takes about two minutes, and is the only part that happens outside this page.

How to create the Twitch app (step by step)
  1. Open dev.twitch.tv/console/apps/create and log in with your normal Twitch account. If it asks you to enable two-factor authentication first, do that in your Twitch security settings and come back.
  2. Name: anything you like, e.g. My Stream Overlay.
  3. OAuth Redirect URLs: paste exactly this — it must match to the letter, including http (not https) and no slash at the end:
    http://localhost:8787/oauth/callback
  4. Category: Application Integration. Then click Create.
  5. On the app's page, copy the Client ID into the field below.
  6. Click New Secret, confirm, and copy the secret it shows into the field below. Twitch only shows a secret once — if you lose it, just make a new one.
Saved into twitch-config.json next to the app.

2Connect your Twitch account

This opens Twitch's own consent page in this tab. You approve it there, Twitch sends your browser straight back here, and the app stores the keys itself. You never see or paste a token — and it renews itself from then on.

4Add the overlays to OBS

Each one is its own Browser Source in OBS (Sources → + → Browser). Paste the URL, set the size, done. The customizer can restyle any of them later without touching OBS again.

Now playing (music) http://127.0.0.1:8787/ 600 × 200
Follow / sub alerts http://127.0.0.1:8787/alerts 1920 × 1080, over everything
Goal boxes http://127.0.0.1:8787/stats the customizer shows the exact size
